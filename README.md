# Lab-13 This lab focuses on creating accurate, engaging, and compliant marketing content for aviation campaigns. It demonstrates how a single verified marketing brief can be adapted for different platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram, advertisements, and promotional emails while keeping all factual information consistent. The lab emphasizes responsible AI-generated marketing by avoiding unsupported claims, clearly communicating eligibility, dates, availability, and applicable conditions, and using fact-difference and register checks. It also includes a compliance checklist, peer review, reflection, and human approval to ensure that the final marketing content is accurate, professional, and not misleading.
